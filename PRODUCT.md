# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This is a private personal-finance workspace for one owner. It is used on a phone for quick monthly check-ins, statement imports, and manual entries, and on a larger screen for deeper analysis. The primary job is to understand where money came from, where it went, and what changed without reconstructing the answer from raw bank exports.

## Product Purpose

Turn bank and credit-card statements plus occasional manual entries into a trustworthy, continuously updated financial history. Success means a completed month can be imported safely, duplicates are prevented, cash in and cash out are immediately legible, and every category can be opened to its underlying transactions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and personal. The app should feel as trustworthy as a bank tool, more understandable than a spreadsheet, and more tailored than a generic budgeting template.

## Anti-references

- A desktop analytics dashboard squeezed into a phone viewport.
- A copied mint-green finance app or a generic navy-and-gold fintech theme.
- Decorative glass, gradients, animation, or cards that compete with financial data.
- Tiny controls, clipped navigation, and color-only status communication.

## Design Principles

- Put cash in, cash out, and net before secondary analytics.
- Make every summary traceable to categories and individual entries.
- Preview financial mutations and make duplicates explicit.
- Use familiar mobile banking patterns and keep deeper tools reachable.
- Preserve one source of truth across imported and manual data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard behavior. Interactive targets are at least 44 by 44 pixels, meaning never depends on color alone, reduced motion is respected, forms have visible labels and errors, and layouts remain usable from 320-pixel phones through desktop widths.
